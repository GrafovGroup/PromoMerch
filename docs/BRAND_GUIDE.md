# PromoMerch Brand Guide

## Selected direction

**1A — Refined Slash** is the selected direction, chosen by the owner “for now.” Use it as the working identity for the website build. This selection replaces the earlier orange PM-monogram exploration. Do not introduce a standalone emblem or reopen unrelated logo concepts.

The primary mark reads **PromoMerch**, without `.ca`. It uses a very heavy geometric sans-serif wordmark: navy **Promo**, cobalt **Merch**, and one rising white cut through the capital **M**. Preserve the reference's proportions, letter relationships, weight, and recognizable M. The cut should remain a simple incision rather than become an arrow, lightning bolt, or swoosh.

The English descriptor is exactly **BRANDED MERCHANDISE**. Position it beneath the wordmark, optically centered, with its outer edges aligned to the lettering above. Adjust tracking carefully; never stretch individual characters to force alignment.

## Colour and typography

| Role | Intended build colour |
|---|---|
| Navy: Promo, primary headings, dark surfaces | `#062A54` |
| Cobalt: Merch, primary accents | `#2454FF` |
| White: backgrounds and reversed artwork | `#FFFFFF` |
| Black: single-colour production treatment | `#000000` |

These are intended brand tokens, not a claim that every pixel in the raster matches these values. Use flat colour in production artwork. Check contrast for each actual text, button, and focus-state combination.

No exact source font has been verified. Do not invent a font identification. Select a suitable licensed geometric sans-serif for interface typography and document it. The custom logo treatment must retain the selected appearance even if its lettering requires careful vector reconstruction. Use readable body typography rather than applying the logo's extreme weight to paragraphs.

## Production asset set

Prepare the following during the authorized build:

- Full colour lockup with English descriptor on a transparent background.
- All-white lockup for dark surfaces, especially the footer, with no visible background box.
- Black monochrome lockup for single-colour reproduction.
- Compact wordmark without descriptor where the full lockup would become illegible.
- Appropriate favicon and touch-icon assets, using a faithful derivative of the distinctive M where necessary at small sizes. This is a functional small-format treatment, not a new primary emblem.

Preserve aspect ratio and consistent clear space. Check actual header, mobile, footer, and icon sizes; do not retain microscopic descriptor text simply to include it everywhere. Test favicon visibility on every route, language, supported browser, and relevant light or dark browser surface.

## English and French

Keep **PromoMerch** unchanged in both languages. The English descriptor is selected. **ARTICLES PROMOTIONNELS** is the proposed French build-localization default and has not yet been owner-approved. Record any later approval or replacement in the decision log. Do not describe the French wording as an already approved brand slogan.

## Website expression

Use a wide, clean, modern layout with strong merchandise imagery, clear content hierarchy, and generous but purposeful spacing. Navigation labels and buttons are uppercase; paragraphs remain naturally cased. Use rounded buttons, visible keyboard focus, and a clear French-language control in the top bar.

Keep decorative arrows, ornamental swooshes, gradients added to the logo, and unrelated emblems out of the design. Use the white footer logo directly on its dark surface. Ensure imagery and copy support product selection and quote requests without inventing completed client work or unverified production capabilities.
