# PromoMerch — Coverage Register

## Scope and page ownership

PromoMerch welcomes merchandise enquiries from all ten Canadian provinces and three territories. This is a national enquiry scope; product availability, assembly, shipping, timing and destination suitability are confirmed for each proposed order. No province-wide delivery guarantee, local office, warehouse, showroom or branch is established by this register.

The single national hub is implemented at [/en/coverage/](https://promomerch.dimagrafov.chatgpt.site/en/coverage/) and [/fr/couverture/](https://promomerch.dimagrafov.chatgpt.site/fr/couverture/). Both are part of the owner-private, noindex review release. There are no separate province, territory or city landing pages.

## Information needed for each order

- Destination postal code and whether delivery is to a business, event venue or another receiving location.
- Selected products, quantities, packaging or assembly needs, and the required in-hand date.
- Fixed receiving windows, venue booking instructions and other known delivery constraints.
- Approximate destination count and regions for multi-destination projects; remote destinations require their own logistics review.
- Recipient address lists are not requested through the general quote form. Detailed recipient information follows an agreed appropriate process if the fulfilment scope is accepted.

## Evidence and publication boundary

The national scope follows the project brief and master prompt, with order-specific qualifications in `lib/content.ts` (content ID `coverage`). It is a business planning and enquiry decision, not evidence of completed deliveries, owned facilities or regional contracts. See [CONTENT_REVIEW.md](CONTENT_REVIEW.md) and [SOURCES_AND_CLAIMS.md](SOURCES_AND_CLAIMS.md).

Keep one useful national hub unless distinct, substantiated regional content and actual service evidence justify a later change. Do not generate city pages from this register or represent the list as a search-indexing record.
